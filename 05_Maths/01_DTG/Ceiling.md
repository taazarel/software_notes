# Ceiling

The ceiling is a [[Function]] from $\mathbb{R}\to \mathbb{Z}$.
The ceiling function assigns each real number to smallest integer that is equal or greater than it. Denoted by $\lceil x \rceil$.

$$
\lceil 2.5 \rceil =3,\quad \lceil -5.1 \rceil = 6,\quad \lceil \pi \rceil =4, \quad \lceil 7 \rceil =7
$$
