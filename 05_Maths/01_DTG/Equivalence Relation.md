# Equivalence Relation

A [[Relation]] R on [[Set]] S is called an [[Equivalence Relation]] if it is: 
1. Reflexive 
2. Transitive  
3. Symmetric[^1]

This is used to find characteristics that elements have in common.

- $ Theorem 1 in 9.5.4 of [Ros] states that point 2 and 3 follow point 1. That means, if (a,b) are [[Equivalent Elements]] then their [[Equivalence Class]]es are equal, and the classes are not [[Disjoint]].
$$
\text{1. }aRb\quad\text{2. }[a]=[b]\quad\text{3. }[a]\cap[b]\neq \varnothing 
$$

Theorem 1 also has the consequence that if two classes are not the same, then they are [[Disjoint]] $[a]_{R}\cap[b]_{R}=\varnothing$ when $[a]_{R}\neq [b]_{R}$.
Therefore; [[Equivalence Class]]es are [[Partition]]s of A.

[^1]: Almost the same as the [[Partial Order]] [[Relation]]s, except for point 3.
