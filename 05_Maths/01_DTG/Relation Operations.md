# Relation Operation

Given two [[Relation]]s R & S from [[Set]] A to [[Set]] B, we can then define new relations based on [[Union of Sets]], and [[Intersection of Sets]].
$$
M_{R\cup S}=M_{R}\lor M_{S}
$$
$$
M_{R\cap S}=M_{R}\land M_{S}
$$

Therefore the new [[Relation]]s will be applying the logical operations on each entry in the matrices.
