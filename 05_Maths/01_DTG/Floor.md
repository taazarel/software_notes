# Floor

The floor is a [[Function]] from $\mathbb{R}\to \mathbb{Z}$.
The floor function assigns each real number to largest integer that is equal or smaller than it. Denoted by $\lfloor x \rfloor$.

$$
\lfloor 2.5 \rfloor =2,\quad \lfloor -5.1 \rfloor = -6,\quad \lfloor \pi \rfloor =3,\quad \lfloor 7 \rfloor =7
$$
