# Logarithmic Functions

The [[Inverse Function]] to the [[Exponent]]ial [[Function]] is with base $b\neq 1$ is called the logarithm to the base $b$, and is denoted as $\log_{b}(x)$.
Its domain are the positive real numbers $\mathbb{R}_{+}$.

$$
\log_{b}(b^{x})=x=b^{\log_{b}(x)}
$$
## Identities
$$
\log_{b}(xy)=\log_{b}(x)+\log_{b}(y)
$$
$$
\log_{b}(x^{y})=y\log_{b}(x)
$$
## Change of Bases
$$
\log_{a}(x)= \frac{\log_{b}(x)}{\log_{b}(a)}
$$
