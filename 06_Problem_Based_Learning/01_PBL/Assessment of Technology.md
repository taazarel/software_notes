# Assessment of Technology

There are a range of different techniques and methods for how to judge a technology and its impact. Here is a list:
- An ethical Perspective: [[QPeTA]]
- A general analysis: [[CTA]]
- A perspective of ecological [[Sustainability]]: [[EIA]]
- A Social Perspective: [[SIA]]
- Judging Risk: [[RA]]
- A Healthy Perspective: [[HTA]]

When is this relevant? Well, that would be; at steps in the progress of making and integrating a new technology. Looking ahead and anticipating consequences of going ahead with progress, is a necessary skill to have.

It might also be relevant when having to choose between use of different technologies. If society changes, we might also have to reevaluate our tech.
