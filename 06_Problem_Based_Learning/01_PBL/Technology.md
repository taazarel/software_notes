# Technology

## What is Technology

We have four criteria for something to be considered technology.
1. Knowledge
2. Organisation
3. Technique
4. Product

## Technology & Society

Technology is also subject to the society which surrounds it. And can in turn affect society. Look at fossil fuels for example. We want to switch, but all of our systems are built on its foundation.
Technologies can also have consequences on other technologies of course. This relationship between different technologies is what makes up the [[Internet of Things]].
