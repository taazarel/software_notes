# Sustainability

Climate change is often in high regard when it comes to [[Sustainability]].

In modern times, having sustainability is a MUST when it comes to making new products. The days where we believed the earths resources were infinite are long since passed. Now we must contend with the reality that our actions as a people has an impact on the planet.

There are three main pillars of sustainability.
1. Economical: Fair trade, circular economy
2. Ecological: Life cycle, 0-sum products
3. Social: Fair trade, Corporate responsibility

- $ Mathematical expression of impact
$$

- P = Population
- A = Affluence (Spending)
